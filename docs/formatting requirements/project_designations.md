# Project designations and title blocks for diploma/course projects

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file applies to diploma projects (`ДП`) and course projects (`КП`), especially their explanatory notes and graphic materials.

## Explanatory Note Sheets

The explanatory note of a diploma project or course project is printed on A4 sheets with a frame and title block.

Frame and clearance rules:

- left sheet edge to frame: 20 mm;
- top, right, and bottom sheet edge to frame: 5 mm;
- distance from frame to text at beginning and end of lines: 5 mm;
- distance from top or bottom text line to frame: 15 mm;
- page number is placed in title-block field 7.

Use `Приложение Р` for the sheet form of the diploma/course project explanatory note.

## Main Title-Block Forms

`Приложение С` gives the required title-block forms:

- form 1 - GOST 2.104 title block for text documents under ESKD, first sheet;
- form 2 - GOST R 21.101 title block for text documents under SPDS, first sheet;
- form 3 - GOST 2.104 title block for text documents under ESKD, subsequent sheets;
- form 4 - GOST R 21.101 title block for text documents under SPDS, subsequent sheets;
- form 5 - GOST 2.104 title block for drawings and schemes under ESKD;
- form 6 - GOST R 21.101 title block for drawings and schemes under SPDS.

For explanatory-note text:

- first page uses form 1 or form 2;
- second and following pages use form 3 or form 4.

For drawings and schemes:

- use form 5 or form 6;
- place title block on the front side in the lower right corner.

## Letter-Numeric Designation

The explanatory note and graphic material of diploma projects and course projects receive a letter-numeric designation according to:

- GOST 2.201;
- or GOST 34.201;
- or GOST R 21.101;
- selected according to specialty.

In the designation structure:

- for GOST 2.201 and GOST 34.201, replace the developer-organization code with the abbreviated work name and, after a dash, the specialty/specialization code;
- for GOST R 21.101, replace the base designation with the abbreviated work name and, after a dash, the specialty/specialization code.

The letter-numeric designation is entered in title-block field 2 on every sheet of the explanatory note and graphic material.

The year of project completion may be included after the specialty/specialization number, separated by a dash.

Examples from the standard:

- `ДП-23.05.02 ХХХХХХ.ХХХ СБ` - assembly drawing in a diploma project;
- `ДП-23.05.02 ХХХХХХ.000 ПЗ` - explanatory note in a diploma project;
- `ДП-08.05.01 КЖ` - reinforced-concrete construction drawings in a diploma project;
- `ДП-08.05.01 ПЗ` - explanatory note in a construction diploma project;
- `ДП-23.05.02-2021 ХХХХХХ.000 ПЗ` - explanatory note with project year;
- `ДП-08.05.01.01-2021 ПЗ` - designation with project year.

## Title-Block Field Meanings

`Приложение С` defines title-block fields:

- field 1 - in explanatory note: diploma/course project topic name according to assignment; on graphic sheets: product name;
- field 2 - diploma/course project designation according to section 9;
- field 3 - enterprise containing the building/structure, district name, or university and institute name; used in construction documentation;
- field 4 - building/structure name or diploma/course project topic; used in construction documentation;
- field 5 - material designation of the part; used on detail drawings;
- field 6 - names of images placed on the sheet, matching drawing image names exactly; used in construction documentation;
- field 7 - drawing sheet ordinal number or text-document page number;
- field 8 - total number of drawing sheets or text-document pages;
- field 9 - name or abbreviation of the department that issued the DP/KP assignment;
- field 10 - role of the person signing the document, such as developed, checked, approved, norm control;
- field 11 - surnames of signers;
- field 12 - signatures of persons named in field 11;
- field 13 - document signing date;
- field 14 - mass of product shown on drawing, under GOST 2.109;
- field 15 - scale under GOST 2.302;
- field 16 - documentation type: `П` for design documentation, `Р` for working documentation; not filled for student projects;
- field 17 - letter; not filled for student projects.
