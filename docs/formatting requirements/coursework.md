# Course projects and course works

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

Common formatting rules are in `docs/common.md`. Graphic and slide rules are in `docs/graphic_and_demonstration_materials.md`. Project designation rules for course projects are in `docs/project_designations.md`.

## Definitions

Course project (`КП`) is an independent work aimed at developing professional skills in solving practical tasks related to the student's specialty. It is completed as:

- a text document/explanatory note;
- graphic material.

Course work (`КР`) is an independent work aimed at developing creative skills, studying the basics of research, and detailed study of one subject-related question. It is completed as a text document.

## Title Page

Use `Приложение И`, mandatory title-page form for course project/course work.

The title page includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- document type: `КУРСОВОЙ ПРОЕКТ` or `КУРСОВАЯ РАБОТА`;
- topic of project/work;
- supervisor signature/date, initials, surname;
- student group number and record book number;
- student signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

## Course Project Specifics

Course project explanatory note is printed on framed sheets with a title block according to `Приложение Р` and `Приложение С`.

Frame and text clearance:

- left sheet border to frame: 20 mm;
- top, right, and bottom sheet border to frame: 5 mm;
- distance from frame to text at beginning/end of lines: 5 mm;
- distance from top or bottom text line to frame: 15 mm;
- page number is placed in field 7 of the title block.

Main title-block rules:

- first text page: form 1 or form 2;
- second and following pages: form 3 or form 4;
- use ESKD forms for ESKD documents and SPDS forms for SPDS documents;
- field 2 contains the project designation from section 9 of the standard.

Course project explanatory note and graphic material receive a letter-numeric designation under section 9. See `docs/project_designations.md`.

## Course Work Specifics

Course work text is printed on sheets without a frame.

Use the non-framed document margins:

- portrait: left 30 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape: left 20 mm, right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

Course work follows the common text structure:

- title page and main part are mandatory;
- contents, introduction, conclusion, list of abbreviations, list of used sources, and appendices are added according to the assignment and department instructions.

If the course work is 24 pages or less, contents are not recommended.

`Приложение Т` is the mandatory example for course-work contents formatting.

## Contents Example Pattern

The course-work contents example in `Приложение Т` shows this order:

- `Введение`;
- numbered sections;
- numbered subsections;
- `Заключение`;
- `Список использованных источников`;
- appendices with designation and title.

Long contents entries continue on the next line at the same text start position. Dot leaders connect headings to page numbers.

## Demonstration Materials

Course work/project demonstration materials may be presented as slides. Slides for defense are printed on A4 as handout material.

Course project drawings, schemes, and posters follow the same graphic rules as VKR graphics. See `docs/graphic_and_demonstration_materials.md`.
