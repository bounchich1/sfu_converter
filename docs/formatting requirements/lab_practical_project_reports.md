# Laboratory, practical, and project-completion reports

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers:

- report on laboratory work;
- report on practical work;
- report on completed project.

Common formatting rules are in `docs/common.md`.

## Title Page

Use `Приложение М`, mandatory title-page form.

The title page includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- one of the document names:
  - `ОТЧЕТ ПО ЛАБОРАТОРНОЙ РАБОТЕ`;
  - `ОТЧЕТ О ПРАКТИЧЕСКОЙ РАБОТЕ`;
  - `ОТЧЕТ О ВЫПОЛНЕНИИ ПРОЕКТА`;
- topic;
- teacher signature/date, initials, surname;
- student group number and record book number;
- student signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

## Formatting

These reports are printed on sheets without a frame.

Use:

- A4 paper;
- Times New Roman, 14 pt;
- single or 1.5 line spacing;
- first-line paragraph indent 12.5 mm;
- justified alignment.

Margins:

- portrait: left 30 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape: left 20 mm, right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

## Structure

Title page and main part are mandatory.

Depending on the assignment and department methodical instructions, include:

- contents;
- introduction;
- conclusion;
- list of abbreviations;
- list of used sources;
- appendices.

If the document is 24 pages or less, contents are not recommended.

## Tables, Figures, and Formulas

Laboratory and practical reports often contain calculations, tables, graphs, schemes, and formulas. Use the common rules:

- formulas are separated by blank lines and numbered when needed;
- table captions are above tables, left aligned, with `Таблица N - ...`;
- figure captions are below figures, centered, with `Рисунок N - ...`;
- all tables and figures must be referenced in text;
- units must be standardized and consistent;
- borrowed materials require bibliographic references.

## Appendices

Appendices are appropriate for:

- large tables;
- source data;
- measurement protocols;
- equipment descriptions;
- software listings or printouts;
- intermediate calculations;
- large figures or schemes.

Use appendix designation and formatting rules from `docs/common.md`.
