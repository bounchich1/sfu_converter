# Referats, calculation works, control works, and essays

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers:

- standalone referat (`РЕФЕРАТ`);
- calculation-graphic assignment/work (`РАСЧЕТНО-ГРАФИЧЕСКОЕ ЗАДАНИЕ (РАБОТА)`);
- calculation assignment (`РАСЧЕТНОЕ ЗАДАНИЕ`);
- control work (`КОНТРОЛЬНАЯ РАБОТА`);
- essay (`ЭССЕ`).

Common formatting rules are in `docs/common.md`.

## Definitions

Standalone referat is an independent student work containing a short presentation of main information from one or several primary sources after semantic processing. It is presented as a text document.

Calculation-graphic assignment/work is an independent student work connected with performing calculations and building graphic models based on them.

Essay is an independent student reasoning on a professional topic, presented as a text document.

## Title Page

Use `Приложение Н`, mandatory title-page form.

The title page includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- one of the document names:
  - `РЕФЕРАТ`;
  - `РАСЧЕТНО-ГРАФИЧЕСКОЕ ЗАДАНИЕ (РАБОТА)`;
  - `РАСЧЕТНОЕ ЗАДАНИЕ`;
  - `КОНТРОЛЬНАЯ РАБОТА`;
  - `ЭССЕ`;
- discipline name after `по`;
- topic or variant;
- teacher signature/date, initials, surname;
- student group number and record book number;
- student signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

## Formatting

These documents are printed on sheets without a frame.

Use:

- A4 paper;
- Times New Roman, 14 pt;
- single or 1.5 line spacing;
- first-line paragraph indent 12.5 mm;
- justified alignment.

Margins:

- portrait: left 30 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape: left 20 mm, right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

## Structure

Title page and main part are mandatory.

Other elements are included according to document content, assignment, and department methodical instructions:

- contents;
- introduction;
- conclusion;
- list of abbreviations;
- list of used sources;
- appendices.

If the document is 24 pages or less, contents are not recommended.

## Referat Notes

For a standalone referat, the standard defines the document type but does not prescribe a separate universal structure beyond common text-document rules.

Use sources carefully:

- include every used printed/electronic source in the source list;
- cite borrowed quotations, formulas, tables, and illustrations;
- use intratext references, footnotes, or source-list references as allowed by the common rules.

## Calculation and Calculation-Graphic Notes

Calculation-graphic work may include:

- calculations;
- formulas;
- tables;
- graphs;
- schemes;
- graphic models;
- appendices with intermediate calculations or large tables.

Use formula, table, illustration, and appendix rules from `docs/common.md`.

## Control Work and Essay Notes

Control works and essays follow common text-document rules. Their content and volume are determined by department methodical instructions and the concrete assignment.

For essays, maintain clear, logical, unambiguous text on the professional topic, with standardized terminology and units where relevant.
