# Graphic and demonstration materials

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers separate graphic and demonstration materials used with VKR and course projects/works. Rules for illustrations inside a text document are in `docs/common.md`.

## Definitions

Graphic material includes drawings, technological and functional schemes, graphs, sections, and other images presented on paper.

Demonstration material includes VKR and course work/project materials presented as slides and also as 3D volumetric models.

Poster (`плакат`) is an image with graphic, textual, and/or photographic information on paper.

## Drawings and Schemes

Drawings and schemes are made on paper. Sheet formats must comply with GOST 2.301.

Each sheet:

- has a frame;
- has a title block;
- uses title block form 5 or form 6 from `Приложение С`;
- places the title block on the front side in the lower right corner.

When preparing drawings, follow the appropriate standards:

- ESKD;
- SPDS;
- ESTD;
- ESPD;
- mining graphic documentation standards, where relevant.

Other drawing rules:

- choose optimal scales under GOST 2.302 considering complexity and information density;
- use standard drawing font under GOST 2.304;
- abbreviations on drawings and specifications follow GOST 2.316 and GOST R 21.101.

## Posters

Posters are made on A1 sheets, 594x841 mm.

Requirements:

- sheet fill density must be at least 70 percent;
- sheets must have names and ordinal numbers written in large font;
- title block uses form 5 or form 6 from `Приложение С`;
- title block is placed on the reverse side of the sheet;
- colored text and images are allowed.

## Slides

Slide requirements:

- first slide contains full university name;
- first slide contains institute name;
- first slide contains work topic;
- first slide contains student full name and supervisor full name;
- first slide contains city and year;
- slide elements such as drawings, graphs, diagrams, and formulas must be clear;
- slide fill density must be at least 70 percent;
- strict style is recommended;
- every slide must be numbered;
- every slide must have a heading;
- if all information does not fit on one slide, move it to the next slide and keep the same heading.

Slides are printed on A4 sheets for use as handout material during defense of VKR and course works.

## Folding and Storage

After VKR defense, drawings and posters are folded to A4 according to `Приложение Ф` and transferred to the graduating department for storage together with the text document/explanatory note.

`Приложение Ф` gives folding sequences for:

- A1, 594x841 mm;
- A2, 420x594 mm;
- A3, 297x420 mm.

The fold order is shown by numbers on the fold lines in the appendix schemes.

## Title Blocks for Graphic Materials

`Приложение С` includes:

- form 5 - title block under GOST 2.104 for drawings and schemes covered by ESKD;
- form 6 - title block under GOST R 21.101 for drawings and schemes covered by SPDS.

Title-block field meanings are summarized in `docs/project_designations.md`.
