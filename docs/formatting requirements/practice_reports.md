# Practice reports

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers:

- report on scientific-pedagogical practice;
- report on production practice;
- report on educational practice;
- general practice report.

Common formatting rules are in `docs/common.md`.

## Title Page

Use `Приложение К`, mandatory title-page form for practice reports.

The title page includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- one of the document names:
  - `ОТЧЕТ О НАУЧНО-ПЕДАГОГИЧЕСКОЙ ПРАКТИКЕ`;
  - `ОТЧЕТ О ПРОИЗВОДСТВЕННОЙ ПРАКТИКЕ`;
  - `ОТЧЕТ ОБ УЧЕБНОЙ ПРАКТИКЕ`;
  - `ОТЧЕТ О ПРАКТИКЕ`;
- place of practice;
- topic;
- university supervisor signature/date, initials, surname;
- enterprise supervisor signature/date, initials, surname;
- student group number and record book number;
- student signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

## Formatting

Practice reports are printed on sheets without a frame.

Use the non-framed document margins:

- portrait: left 30 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape: left 20 mm, right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

Use common text style:

- Times New Roman, 14 pt;
- single or 1.5 line spacing;
- 12.5 mm first-line indent;
- justified alignment.

## Structure

Title page and main part are mandatory.

Other sections are included according to the practice assignment and department methodical instructions:

- contents;
- introduction;
- conclusion;
- list of abbreviations;
- list of used sources;
- appendices.

If the report is 24 pages or less, contents are not recommended.

## Typical Appendix Materials

Practice reports may use appendices for:

- acts, protocols, and supporting documents;
- large tables or figures;
- data collected during practice;
- printouts;
- additional schemes, forms, and calculations;
- implementation or test acts when relevant.

Appendices follow the common appendix rules in `docs/common.md`.
