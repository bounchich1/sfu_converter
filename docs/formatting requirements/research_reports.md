# Research-work reports

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers reports on scientific research work, especially the master's research-work report form in `Приложение Л`.

Common formatting rules are in `docs/common.md`.

## Definition

Student research work includes:

- study of theoretical foundations of research methods;
- formulation, organization, and execution of scientific research;
- planning and organization of scientific experiments;
- processing of scientific data;
- assignments, laboratory works, course works, and VKR containing research elements.

## Title Page for Master's Research-Work Report

Use `Приложение Л`, mandatory title-page form for `ОТЧЕТ О НАУЧНО-ИССЛЕДОВАТЕЛЬСКОЙ РАБОТЕ` of a master's student.

The title page includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- approval by master program head;
- master program head signature, initials, surname, and approval date;
- document name `ОТЧЕТ О НАУЧНО-ИССЛЕДОВАТЕЛЬСКОЙ РАБОТЕ`;
- code and name of master program;
- topic;
- supervisor signature/date, initials, surname;
- student group number and record book number;
- student signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

## Formatting

Research-work reports are printed on sheets without a frame.

Use:

- A4 paper;
- Times New Roman, 14 pt;
- single or 1.5 line spacing;
- 12.5 mm first-line indent;
- justified alignment;
- portrait margins: left 30 mm, top/bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape margins: left/right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

## Structure

Title page and main part are mandatory.

Other elements are included when required by the assignment or department methodical instructions:

- contents;
- introduction;
- conclusion;
- list of abbreviations;
- list of used sources;
- appendices.

Research reports commonly use tables, figures, formulas, sources, and appendices. Apply the detailed common rules for those elements from `docs/common.md`.

## Research Content Notes

The standard does not set a fixed universal content plan or volume for research reports. Content and volume are determined by department methodological instructions and the concrete assignment.

Appendices may include:

- experimental data;
- descriptions of instruments and apparatus;
- intermediate proofs and calculations;
- protocols;
- test acts;
- patent research report;
- software printouts or source code;
- large tables and illustrations.
