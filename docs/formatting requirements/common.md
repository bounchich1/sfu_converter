# Common formatting rules from STU 7.5-07-2021

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`, Siberian Federal University standard "General requirements for structure, presentation, and formatting of educational documents".

## Scope

The standard applies to student educational documents submitted on paper or in the electronic educational environment. Its requirements are mandatory for students and for instructors performing norm control.

Document families covered by the standard:

- выпускные квалификационные работы (VKR: bachelor work, diploma project, diploma work, master dissertation);
- course projects and course works;
- control works;
- calculation-graphic assignments/works and calculation assignments;
- reports on all types of practice, laboratory work, and practical work;
- research-work reports;
- project-completion reports;
- standalone abstracts/referats;
- essays.

Student documents are divided into text documents and graphic material. Text documents include master dissertations, diploma works, bachelor works, course works, explanatory notes for diploma/course projects, control works, referats, reports, text parts of calculation-graphic assignments/works, and essays.

Content requirements and document volume are set by department methodological instructions for the relevant field, specialty, and concrete assignment.

## Core Text Document Structure

In the general case a text document consists of:

- title page;
- abstract/referat;
- contents;
- introduction;
- main part;
- conclusion;
- list of abbreviations;
- list of used sources;
- appendices.

The title page and main part are mandatory for every text document. Other elements are included according to document type, content, department methodical documents, or the assignment.

Each structural element starts on a new page.

Structural-element headings use these rules:

- headings such as `РЕФЕРАТ`, `АННОТАЦИЯ`, `СОДЕРЖАНИЕ`, `ВВЕДЕНИЕ`, `ЗАКЛЮЧЕНИЕ`, `СПИСОК СОКРАЩЕНИЙ`, `СПИСОК ИСПОЛЬЗОВАННЫХ ИСТОЧНИКОВ`, `ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ` are centered;
- no paragraph indent;
- uppercase letters;
- bold font;
- no numbering;
- no underline;
- separated from the following text by one blank line.

## Title Page

The title page is the first page of the text document. It is included in the total page count, but the page number is not printed on it.

The exact title-page form depends on the document type. Use the corresponding appendix form:

- `Приложение Б` - master dissertation title page;
- `Приложение В` - diploma project title page;
- `Приложение Г` - diploma work title page;
- `Приложение Д` - bachelor work title page;
- `Приложение И` - course project/course work title page;
- `Приложение К` - practice report title page;
- `Приложение Л` - master's research-work report title page;
- `Приложение М` - laboratory work, practical work, and project-completion report title page;
- `Приложение Н` - referat, calculation-graphic assignment/work, calculation assignment, control work, essay title page.

Common title-page data includes, where applicable to the selected form:

- university name;
- full institute name;
- full department name;
- approval block;
- full document type/name;
- specialty, specialization, direction, or master program code and name for VKR;
- topic name according to the assignment;
- position, academic degree, surname, initials, and signature of the supervisor;
- surname, initials, and signature of the student/performer;
- city and year.

## Contents

If the document is 24 pages or less, including `Содержание` is not recommended.

The contents section includes:

- headings of structural elements;
- ordinal numbers and headings of all sections, subsections, and points;
- designations and headings of appendices;
- page numbers where each item starts.

Formatting rules for contents:

- headings are written in lowercase with the first letter uppercase;
- after each heading, put dot leaders and the page number;
- contents headings must exactly match headings in the text;
- do not shorten headings or reword them;
- section numbers/headings and structural-element headings start at the line beginning;
- subsection entries are indented by two characters relative to section entries;
- point entries are indented by two characters relative to subsection entries;
- continuation lines start at the same level as the first line of that heading;
- appendix heading continuation lines start at the level of the appendix designation;
- all appendices may be grouped under one `Приложения` entry with appendix range and page interval.

`Приложение Т` gives a mandatory example of contents for a course work.

## Introduction, Main Part, Conclusion

The introduction generally contains:

- current state of the researched problem;
- goal and tasks;
- methods and means used to solve tasks;
- relevance and novelty of the work.

The main part depends on the topic and document type. It may include:

- theoretical questions;
- work methods;
- experimental studies;
- patent-information search results;
- calculations;
- graphs;
- tables;
- schemes.

The conclusion may include:

- conclusions based on work results;
- assessment of completeness of task solutions and obtained results;
- advantages of accepted solutions and recommendations for use;
- technical-economic efficiency of implementation/application;
- theoretical and practical value of obtained results.

## List of Abbreviations

Use this section for abbreviations that are used in the text and are not established by standards.

Rules:

- arrange abbreviations in a column;
- left side: abbreviations in alphabetical order;
- right side: full decoding;
- place this section near the end of the document before the list of used sources.

When a custom abbreviation system is used, decode each abbreviation at first mention in the text and also include it in `СПИСОК СОКРАЩЕНИЙ`.

Example pattern: `информационно-аналитический комплекс (ИАК)`.

Do not use word/phrase abbreviations in figure captions, figure headings, table captions, section headings, subsection headings, or point headings.

## List of Used Sources

The list includes all printed and electronic publications used while writing the work.

Placement and formatting:

- place at the end of the text document before appendices;
- bibliographic records follow GOST R 7.0.100 and GOST 7.80;
- allowed word reductions must follow GOST 7.11 and GOST R 7.0.12;
- all records are numbered with Arabic numerals;
- print records with paragraph indent;
- numbering is continuous through the whole list.

Allowed grouping methods:

- alphabetical;
- systematic/thematic, in order of mention in the text;
- chronological.

Use one grouping method consistently. Do not mix grouping methods.

For sources in languages other than Russian, create an additional alphabetical sequence after Russian-language sources.

`Приложение У` gives examples for legal acts, standards, patents, one-author books, multi-author books, multivolume editions, dissertations and dissertation abstracts, electronic resources, journal articles, serial publications, collections, conference proceedings, and book chapters.

## Page and Paper Setup

Text documents are printed with a computer and printer on one side of white A4 paper (210x297 mm).

General text style:

- font: Times New Roman, 14 pt;
- line spacing: single or 1.5;
- paragraph first-line indent: 12.5 mm, equal through the whole document;
- alignment: justified to left and right margins.

For text documents without a frame, use these margins:

- portrait orientation: left 30 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm, right 10 mm;
- landscape orientation: left 20 mm, right 20 mm, top 30 mm, bottom 10 mm.

Exceptionally, handwritten text is allowed. It must be clear and neat, in one ink color, with letters and digits at least 2.5 mm high, and line spacing from 8 to 10 mm.

## Page Numbering

Number all pages with Arabic numerals using continuous numbering through the whole document.

For pages without a frame:

- page number is centered at the bottom of the page;
- no paragraph indent;
- Times New Roman, 14 pt.

For pages with a frame and title block:

- page number is placed in field 7 of the title block.

The title page is counted but has no printed page number.

## Text Style and Terminology

Text must be clear, logical, unambiguous, necessary, and sufficient to understand the document topic.

Use scientific and technical terms, designations, and definitions established by the relevant standards. If no standard term exists, use terms accepted in scientific and technical literature.

Conditional letter symbols, images, and signs must correspond to state standards. If non-standard symbols/signs are used, explain them in the text.

Use standardized physical units, names, and designations according to GOST 8.417. The same parameter must use the same unit throughout the document.

## Division of Text

Divide the main part into sections and subsections. If needed, divide sections/subsections into points and subpoints.

Each point or subpoint must contain complete information.

Numbering rules:

- sections are numbered with Arabic numerals within the main part: `1`, `2`, `3`;
- subsections are numbered within each section: `1.1`, `1.2`;
- if a section has no subsections, point numbers consist of section number and point number;
- points are numbered within each subsection: `1.1.1`, `1.1.2`;
- subpoints are numbered within each point: `1.1.1.1`, `1.1.1.2`;
- section, subsection, point, and subpoint numbers are printed with paragraph indent.

Enumeration rules:

- normal lists use paragraph indent and a hyphen before each item;
- if the text needs references to list items, use lowercase Russian letters with a closing parenthesis: `а)`, `б)`;
- for deeper detail, use Arabic numerals with a closing parenthesis: `1)`, `2)`;
- nested numeric list markers are shifted two characters to the right relative to the lettered list.

## Headings

Sections and subsections of the main part must have headings.

Point headings are used when a subsection contains two or more points divided into subpoints. In that case, headings are given for all points in the subsection.

Heading rules:

- heading must briefly and clearly reflect the content;
- print after the section/subsection/point number;
- first letter uppercase;
- bold font;
- no period at the end;
- no underline;
- no word hyphenation;
- if a heading has two sentences, separate them with a period;
- separate headings from text by one blank line.

## Formulas

Formula rules:

- place formulas on a separate line with paragraph indent;
- leave one blank line above and below each formula;
- if a formula does not fit on one line, break it at an operation sign and repeat that sign at the beginning of the next line;
- number formulas in Arabic numerals in parentheses at the far right of the line, aligned with the formula;
- formula numbering may be continuous through the document or within each section;
- section-based numbers consist of section number and formula number, separated by a period, for example `(1.1)`;
- formulas inside tables or explanatory data for illustrations are not numbered;
- appendix formulas use separate numbering with the appendix letter before the number, for example `(А.1)`;
- explain symbols and numeric coefficients directly under the formula;
- each symbol explanation starts on a new line;
- the first explanation line starts with `где`, without a colon and without paragraph indent;
- repeated symbols are explained once at first use; later references may say that the symbol is the same as in a previous formula;
- consecutive formulas not separated by text are separated by commas;
- references to formulas use the formula number in parentheses, for example `по формуле (2)`;
- formulas and symbols may be handwritten in black paste if they comply with GOST 2.304.

## Tables

Use tables for clearer presentation and comparison of numeric or textual material.

Placement:

- place a table directly after the text where it is referenced, or on the next page;
- if needed, place a table in an appendix;
- tables may be placed along the long side of the sheet in landscape orientation.

Caption and structure:

- above the table, aligned left, without paragraph indent, print `Таблица`, table number, dash, and table name;
- table name starts with an uppercase letter;
- no period after the table name;
- table name must be accurate, short, and reflect table content;
- table caption font size: 14 pt;
- table is bounded by lines on the left, right, and bottom;
- table head is separated from the rest of the table by a double line;
- do not include a separate `Номер по порядку` column.

Numbering and references:

- tables, except appendix tables, are numbered with Arabic numerals using continuous numbering;
- section-based table numbering is allowed, for example `Таблица 7.1`;
- appendix tables use appendix letter plus number, for example `Таблица А.1`;
- every table must be referenced in the text.

Header rules:

- column and row headings start with uppercase letters;
- column subheadings start lowercase if they continue the heading sentence, uppercase if independent;
- column headings are bold and centered;
- headings are normally parallel to table rows; perpendicular placement is allowed if necessary;
- do not separate headings/subheadings and side headings with diagonal lines;
- no periods at the end of table headings and subheadings.

Units and numeric material:

- if all table indicators use one unit, put the unit designation once above the table on the right in 12 pt;
- if columns use different units, put units in each column or row heading after the indicator name, separated by a comma;
- if indicators need numbering, put ordinal numbers in the first side-heading column before the indicator names;
- small numeric material may be placed in the text as aligned columns instead of a table.

Large or continued tables:

- if rows do not fit on a page, split the table and place parts side by side or on following pages;
- write table number and name only above the first part;
- above following parts write `Продолжение таблицы ...`;
- above the last part write `Окончание таблицы ...`;
- repeat the table head on continuation pages;
- it is allowed to replace the head on continuation pages with a numbering row for side heading and columns, provided that row appears on the first page after the head.

Other table details:

- abbreviated parameter names may be replaced by letter designations from GOST 2.321 or by other designations explained in the text or graphics;
- letter designations are italicized;
- table footnotes use superscript `*` or Arabic numerals with `)`;
- table footnotes are placed at the end of the table above the closing line;
- recommended table font: Times New Roman, 10-12 pt.

## Illustrations

Illustrations include drawings, diagrams, graphs, photographs, and schemes.

Placement and readability:

- place an illustration directly after its first reference or on the next page;
- if inserted in a text break, center it relative to page margins;
- leave one blank line above and below it;
- it must occupy enough space to be clearly visible and readable;
- lines and labels must be clear.

Numbering:

- use the word `Рисунок`;
- number illustrations with Arabic numerals using continuous numbering;
- section-based numbering is allowed;
- if the document has one illustration, designate it `Рисунок 1`;
- appendix illustrations are numbered within each appendix, for example `Рисунок А.1`.

Caption and explanatory data:

- illustrations must have a name;
- explanatory data is added when needed;
- explanatory data is placed under the illustration;
- below the explanatory data, centered, print `Рисунок`, number, dash, and name in Times New Roman 14 pt;
- recommended font for explanatory data: Times New Roman 12 pt.

Multi-page illustrations:

- on the first page, include illustration number and name with `лист 1`;
- on following pages, include only number and sheet number, for example `Рисунок 1, лист 2`;
- explanatory data is placed on the relevant sheet or on the last sheet above the figure number.

Other rules:

- drawings included in a text document must follow current ESKD/SPDS standards;
- choose the diagram type that matches the visualized data;
- references use wording such as `в соответствии с рисунком 2` or `на рисунке 3`.

## Bibliographic References in Text

When using material from other documents, including formulas, tables, quotations, or illustrations, give a bibliographic reference according to GOST R 7.0.5.

Allowed reference types:

- intratext reference in round parentheses directly after the relevant text;
- footnote reference at the bottom of the page;
- reference to the numbered list of used sources in square brackets.

Footnote reference rules:

- separate the footnote area from the text with a short thin horizontal line on the left;
- print footnotes in reduced font size;
- use superscript Arabic numerals as footnote marks;
- a superscript asterisk is allowed instead of numerals;
- include full bibliographic description, or only the source details not already present in the text.

References to the source list:

- use square brackets with source number, for example `[13]`;
- for exact fragments, add page after comma, for example `[20, с. 29]`;
- for multivolume sources, add volume/issue/part data, for example `[18, т. 1, с. 75]`;
- for multiple works or shared ideas, separate source groups with semicolons, for example `[59; 67, с. 40-46; 82]`.

## Appendices

Appendices contain material that supplements the document.

Possible appendix materials include:

- large-format or high-volume illustrations;
- estimates and statements;
- descriptions of apparatus and instruments used in experiments, measurements, or tests;
- auxiliary illustrations;
- intermediate mathematical proofs, formulas, and calculations;
- protocols, implementation acts, equipment test acts, patent research report;
- questionnaires;
- computer printouts;
- software source texts developed during the work;
- tables with data supplementing main results;
- list of completed graphic material;
- borrowed schemes, drawings, statements, specifications, and tables from other independently issued documents.

Formatting:

- place appendices at the end of the text document;
- designate appendices with uppercase Russian letters beginning with `А`;
- do not use letters `Ё`, `З`, `Й`, `О`, `Ч`, `Ь`, `Ы`, `Ъ`;
- write the letter after the word `ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ`;
- start each appendix on a new page;
- each appendix has a heading;
- appendix heading starts with uppercase letter, is bold, centered, and separated from text by one blank line;
- appendices are usually A4, but A3, A3x4, A4x4, A2, and A1 are allowed under GOST 2.301.

Appendix internals:

- sections/subsections/points inside an appendix may be numbered within that appendix;
- prefix appendix section numbers with the appendix designation;
- if an appendix table continues over pages, write `Продолжение приложения` or `Окончание приложения`, add its designation, leave one blank line, repeat the table head, and continue the table.

Appendices may be continued pages of the same document or separate documents. Continued appendices use the same continuous page numbering as the main document.

When referencing appendices, use wording such as `в соответствии с приложением ...` or `приведен в приложении ...`.

If an appendix is a separate independent document, insert it into the text document. On its title page, under the document name, write `ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ` and its designation. Its pages are included in the total page numbering of the text document.
