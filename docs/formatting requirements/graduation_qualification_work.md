# Graduation qualification works (VKR)

Source: `docs/sfu-stu-7.5-07.pdf`.

This file covers выпускная квалификационная работа (VKR), including:

- бакалаврская работа (BR);
- дипломный проект (DP);
- дипломная работа (DR);
- магистерская диссертация (MD).

Common text-document rules are in `docs/common.md`. Graphic and slide rules are in `docs/graphic_and_demonstration_materials.md`. Project designation rules for diploma projects are in `docs/project_designations.md`.

## Definitions

VKR is the final attestation work completed after theoretical and practical training.

Bachelor work is prepared in the final period of theoretical training and may be an independent study or a generalization of earlier course projects/works.

Diploma project/work is a final project or theoretical work confirming the graduate's competence, knowledge, and skills. It is presented as a text document and graphic material.

Master dissertation is a final qualification work performed as an independent scientific study under the guidance of the master's research supervisor.

## VKR Assignment

The composition of a VKR is established by the VKR assignment.

The assignment:

- is issued individually to each student;
- follows the recommended form in `Приложение А`;
- is inserted into the text document after the title page;
- is not included in the total page numbering of the text document.

`Приложение А` assignment data includes:

- institute and department;
- approval by department head;
- VKR form: bachelor work, diploma project, diploma work, or master dissertation;
- student full name;
- group number;
- direction/specialty code and full name;
- VKR topic;
- university order number and date approving the topic;
- supervisor initials, surname, position, academic title, and workplace;
- source data for VKR;
- list of VKR sections;
- list of graphic material;
- supervisor signature;
- student acceptance signature and date.

## Norm Control and Storage

VKR must undergo mandatory norm control according to STU 7.5-10.

After defense, drawings and posters are folded to A4 format according to `Приложение Ф` and transferred to the graduating department for storage together with the text document or explanatory note.

## VKR Title Pages

Use the mandatory appendix matching the VKR type:

- `Приложение Б` - master dissertation;
- `Приложение В` - diploma project;
- `Приложение Г` - diploma work;
- `Приложение Д` - bachelor work;
- `Приложение Е` - additional VKR title-page continuation page;
- `Приложение Ж` - VKR title page with signatures of reviewer, consultant, and norm controller.

Title page generally includes:

- Ministry and university block;
- institute;
- department;
- approval by department head;
- VKR type;
- topic;
- specialty/direction/program code and name as required by the form;
- supervisor signature/date, position, academic degree, initials, surname;
- graduate signature/date, initials, surname;
- city and year.

For master dissertations and diploma works, the title page may include reviewer surname, initials, signature, position, and academic degree.

If VKR has consultants by sections and a norm controller, their agreement is placed on the additional title-page page from `Приложение Е`.

If the number of VKR consultants is no more than two, reviewer, consultants, and norm-controller data may be placed directly on the title page using `Приложение Ж`.

## Master Dissertation Specifics

Master dissertation may contain additional sections, including:

- annotation;
- author's abstract / autoreferat.

These are governed by the separate Regulation on the Master Dissertation referenced in the standard.

The master dissertation title page includes:

- topic;
- code and name of direction;
- code and name of master program;
- supervisor;
- graduate;
- reviewer.

## Diploma Project Specifics

Diploma project includes:

- title page from the diploma project appendix form;
- explanatory note (`Пояснительная записка`);
- graphic material as required by the assignment.

The explanatory note of a diploma project is printed on framed sheets with a title block. The frame, title block forms, distances, and field filling rules are covered in `docs/project_designations.md`.

Diploma project text and graphic materials receive a letter-numeric designation according to section 9 of the standard.

## Diploma Work Specifics

Diploma work title page includes:

- code and name of specialty;
- topic;
- supervisor;
- graduate;
- reviewer.

Diploma work uses the common text-document formatting rules for non-framed documents unless another department/specialty rule requires otherwise.

## Bachelor Work Specifics

Bachelor work title page includes:

- code and name of direction;
- topic;
- supervisor;
- graduate.

Bachelor work uses the common text-document formatting rules for non-framed documents unless another department/specialty rule requires otherwise.

## VKR Abstract (`Реферат ВКР`)

The VKR abstract is a short, exact presentation of the VKR content. It includes major facts and conclusions without additional interpretation or critical comments.

The abstract must contain:

- VKR topic name;
- number of text-document pages;
- number of illustrations;
- number of tables;
- number of formulas;
- number of appendices;
- number of used sources;
- number of graphic-material sheets;
- list of keywords;
- abstract text.

Keyword rules:

- not more than 15 words or word combinations;
- chosen from the VKR text;
- in nominative case;
- printed in uppercase;
- written in one line separated by commas.

The abstract text must reflect:

- goal and tasks;
- relevance;
- novelty;
- effectiveness;
- conclusions;
- recommendations for practical implementation of results in production, research, or education.

The text must be as brief and informative as possible. Abstract text volume is not more than one page.

`Приложение П` gives a recommended VKR abstract example.

## Graphic and Demonstration Materials for Defense

VKR may include drawings, schemes, posters, slides, and 3D models.

Important rules:

- drawings and schemes follow ESKD/SPDS/ESTD/ESPD or relevant mining graphic documentation standards;
- posters are A1 and at least 70 percent filled;
- slides must have a strict style, clear elements, numbering, and headings;
- first slide includes university, institute, topic, student and supervisor names, city, and year;
- slides must be printed on A4 for use as handouts during defense.

See `docs/graphic_and_demonstration_materials.md` for the full checklist.
